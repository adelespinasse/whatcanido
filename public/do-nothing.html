Do nothing

Please don’t do literally nothing. But if you are going to end up doing very little, I suggest you commit to it, and don’t stress. It’s very easy to stress yourself out trying to figure out the best way to help, without actually finding much. (Or at least, it’s easy for me; that is what eventually led to this article.)

My best suggestion is: do a little bit of work, as early as possible in the election season, to figure out what you can do. For a few people, it might end up being your full-time occupation for campaign season, or even your life calling. But for most people, it may not be easy to find much that’s worth doing (except, probably, voting and donating; and maybe talking to friends and family). Take that as a gift, not a challenge. Do your bit, and then live your life.
